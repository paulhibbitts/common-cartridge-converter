Convert a Common Cartridge (.imscc) export to Markdown pages for Grav Helios Course Hub

A modern, clean home for your course, yours to keep. Grav Helios Course Hub gives you direct access to the content behind it: portable, future-friendly Markdown files, no database, platform independence.

If needed, embed any page into Canvas, Moodle, or Brightspace with a URL parameter, and use your LMS as a partner for assignments and grading, with your content always directly accessible whenever you need it.

For open publishers and OER-minded educators, your course is open and publicly accessible on the web by default, and the Markdown files themselves can also be made available for others to reuse and remix.

Evaluating Grav Helios Course Hub? Convert one of your own courses and browse the resulting Markdown files and images directly: no Grav or Helios installation needed to see what carries over.

What exactly does this do?

It's a self-contained PHP web app running on the server. You upload a Common Cartridge .imscc export, it parses the module structure and converts wiki page content to Markdown, then hands you back a ZIP ready to drop into Grav.

What's converted?

  1. Course modules → chapter landing pages with numbered child pages
  2. Wiki pages → course-page.md Markdown files with YAML frontmatter
  3. Module section headers (ContextModuleSubHeader) → group: frontmatter for visual grouping in the Course Hub sidebar
  4. Vimeo and YouTube iframes → [iframe] and [youtube] shortcodes
  5. A module.md card-grid listing page for all modules

What's not included?
Quizzes, discussions, and assignments have no equivalent in Helios Course Hub and are not included. A full count appears in conversion-notes.txt in the ZIP.

What happens to your file?
The .imscc is extracted to a server temp directory, parsed, converted, and packaged. The temp files are immediately deleted after the ZIP is streamed to your browser — nothing is stored on the server.

Uploaded files are processed in a temporary directory and deleted immediately after conversion. Nothing is stored on the server.
